# Third-party notices

VRCLibMan does not bundle third-party word lists. Versions up to v0.6.3 bundled two lists for the removed Apple on-device classifier: `Sexual.txt` from [inappropriate-words-ja](https://github.com/MosasoM/inappropriate-words-ja) (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020 K Hashimoto) and `en` from [List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words) (CC-BY-4.0). Those versions keep the notices they shipped with.

The bundled avatar catalog has its own source and terms in [mochifitter-profiles.SOURCE.md](src-tauri/knowledge/mochifitter-profiles.SOURCE.md).
